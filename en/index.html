THE COMMUNITY

A faith to live.
A life to share.

Amid the pace of Madrid, we gather to return to what matters: Jesus, His Word and love for our neighbors.

We are Christians seeking to follow the Way of Jesus Christ and the example of the first-century church found in the New Testament. We want to grow in faith and encourage one another. We are Madrid Church of Christ, meeting near Las Rosas Metro for worship and Bible study.

Whether you live here or are passing through, know the Bible or have your first questions, you are welcome.

HOW WE WORSHIP

Simple worship.
Guided by Scripture.

Jesus teaches us to worship the Father in spirit and in truth. His words and the teaching and example of the first-century church guide how we worship together.

John 4:23-24 ↗
01 /

Learning from Scripture

The first Christians devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching. We read and study the Bible so its message guides our faith and our lives, helping us grow together in our knowledge of God.

Acts 2:42 ↗2 Timothy 3:16-17 ↗
02 /

Praying together

Prayer was part of the life of the first church. We thank God, bring our needs before Him and pray for one another, seeking a life of continual prayer.

Acts 2:42 ↗1 Thessalonians 5:16-18 ↗
03 /

Singing from the heart

We sing together a cappella, without instruments. We seek to follow the New Testament teaching to praise God from the heart and to teach and encourage one another with psalms, hymns and spiritual songs.

Ephesians 5:19 ↗Colossians 3:16 ↗
04 /

The Lord’s Supper

We share the Lord’s Supper each Sunday, following the example of the disciples who gathered on the first day of the week to break bread. With the bread and the cup, we remember Jesus’ sacrifice, proclaim His death and examine ourselves.

Acts 20:7 ↗1 Corinthians 11:23-29 ↗
05 /

Giving freely and gladly

Paul instructed churches to set aside gifts on the first day of the week to help believers in need. His teaching guides our giving: a personal, generous choice made without pressure. Visitors are not expected to contribute.

1 Corinthians 16:1-2 ↗2 Corinthians 9:6-7 ↗
06 /

Building one another up

We gather to encourage love and good works. We want worship to strengthen the community, with everything done respectfully and in order, for everyone’s encouragement.

Hebrews 10:24-25 ↗1 Corinthians 14:26-40 ↗

BEFORE YOU VISIT

There is room
for your questions.

Is this a Catholic church?

No. We are a Church of Christ: a community of Christians seeking to follow the Way of Jesus Christ and the teaching and practice of the first-century church, as recorded in the New Testament. The Bible is our guide. We respect our neighbors of other traditions.

What happens during worship?

We sing a cappella, pray, learn from Scripture, share the Lord’s Supper and give freely. Read how we worship and the passages that guide us ↗.

Do I need to be a member to attend?

No. You can come to meet the community and learn with us, even if this is your first visit to a church.

Can I come if I do not know the Bible?

Yes. Bring your questions and a willingness to listen. You do not need any previous knowledge.

When and where do you meet?

Sunday worship begins at 10:30 a.m., Madrid local time, at Paseo de Ginebra 35, local 2. The Metro stop is Las Rosas, Line 2.

Is there information in English?

Yes. Use ES / EN to read this website in English. Contact Ángel to ask about the language of the gatherings or interpretation arrangements.
